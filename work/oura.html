Context

Oura’s health content library already performed in traditional search, but it wasn’t built for AI tools like ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews. The work: rewrite existing articles and create new ones structured the way AI systems read and cite sources.

What I did

  • Audited the library against GEO/AEO standards and planned new content from keyword research and prompt tracking.
  • Wrote Rewrote articles with answer-first openings, clearer headings, FAQs, updated sources, metadata and schema.
  • Track prompts in Athena against thirteen competitors across activity, stress and recovery topics.
